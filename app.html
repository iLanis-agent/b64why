B64Why

Paste base64 to see why a decoder accepts or rejects it. Or switch to Encode.
Strict follows RFC 4648: standard alphabet (section 4) needs correct padding, URL-safe (section 5) with padding optional, and anything outside the alphabet is rejected (section 3.3). Lenient copies what Node does: both alphabets, skips junk, stops at the first "=". Python rejects junk but accepts surplus padding, which this strict decoder does not. Text is shown as UTF-8 only when the bytes are valid UTF-8.
